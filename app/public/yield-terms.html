Yield vault terms

Updated October 2, 2026 · Operated by Daviswhistle

These terms apply when you choose a Morpho yield vault. The free basic WLD vault follows the general terms. Each asset has its own personal vault, heir and countdown.

What is supplied

A WLD yield vault supplies deposits to Re7 WLD on Morpho. A USDC yield vault supplies World Chain USDC to Re7 USDC on Morpho. USDC principal and interest remain USDC; campaign rewards are WLD. The app does not convert between currencies. The strategy, service fee and fee recipient are fixed in each factory at deployment. Our contracts have no administrator, upgrade or strategy-switch permission. The underlying Morpho vault has separate governance, curator, allocation and fee risks.

Our 10% performance fee

There is no principal or periodic management charge. A cash exit charges 10% of positive realized gains after the underlying vault's costs. A receipt-share exit charges the corresponding positive-gain fee in shares, using their quoted underlying-asset value. A failed valuation waives that share-exit's fee. Deposited capital and direct token or receipt-share gifts are not fee-bearing. Fees already paid are not refunded if later losses occur.

USDC and WLD are accounted separately, without a price oracle or cross-currency offsets. In a USDC vault, realized USDC losses offset later realized USDC gains before a USDC fee is collected. Canonical WLD rewards actually claimed by the vault have no WLD deposited-capital basis and pay a separate 10% WLD fee when distributed. A USDC loss does not offset a WLD reward fee. For example, a 100 USDC deposit exited at 110 USDC pays 1 USDC; 10 claimed WLD rewards pay 1 WLD. WLD gifts pay no fee.

In a WLD yield vault, claimed WLD rewards can be reinvested without increasing deposited capital. Remaining capital and realized WLD losses offset WLD gains before the fee. Partial withdrawals allocate capital and gains proportionally. Receipt shares redeemed later from your wallet have already paid their service fee on leaving the inheritance vault and are not charged a second service fee.

Underlying fees and displayed rates

The underlying Re7 vault currently charges a 10% performance fee and can change its fees and allocations. Our fee is additional and applies after the underlying costs. Displayed lending APR and general WLD rewards APR are separate, dated estimates before our exit fee. Rewards eligibility, exchange values, rate sources and campaign end dates can change. Failed or stale queries remove the rate display without blocking transactions. An annualized reward rate is not a promise of that year's payout.

WLD campaign rewards

Campaign participation is not guaranteed. World App's verified-human boost is not automatic for a personal vault contract and is excluded from our general-rate display. Rewards become claimable only after publication by the canonical Merkl distributor. A reward API cannot choose the token, distributor or payout recipient.

Active WLD yield vaults reinvest available claimed WLD. Active USDC vaults keep claimed WLD separately: the owner can withdraw held WLD while owner controls are available, and a full cash exit also pays it. Partial USDC or receipt-share exits leave WLD in the vault. Reward claims do not renew the countdown. Rewards already delivered by approved external operators follow the same accounting. After inheritance, newly claimed or externally delivered canonical WLD rewards go to the fixed inheritance recipient after the applicable fee; they cannot be reclaimed by the original owner. Empty released vaults remain known to their factory so delayed rewards can still be claimed. Keep the vault link.

Withdrawals and inheritance

Cash withdrawals depend on Morpho liquidity. The owner can move receipt shares instead and redeem them later as liquidity allows. After expiry, the named heir must file a claim and wait seven days. The owner can renew to cancel the claim until execution. An eligible inheritance attempts cash redemption and falls back to receipt shares if redemption fails. The fixed heir receives all idle underlying assets and, in a USDC vault, held WLD rewards after their separate fee. A share payout preserves delivery, not principal value or immediate cash availability. Gas execution limits can cause a share payout even when some liquidity exists.

Risks

Deposited principal can be lost. Contract failures, bad debt, oracle failure, allocation changes, token-price changes and insufficient liquidity can cause partial or total loss or delayed access. USDC can lose its dollar peg or be subject to issuer restrictions; USDC is not a bank deposit. A receipt quote and a displayed rate do not guarantee value or liquidity. Protocol audits do not audit this integration. This integration has no independent third-party security audit.

Your choices and transaction costs

Creating a vault moves no funds. Creation, deposits and owner withdrawals require your wallet approval. Existing WLD vaults are not converted or migrated automatically. You can manage one current vault per supported factory; each has its own balance, heir and countdown. The operator pays automated inheritance execution gas from a dedicated ETH wallet. Review any transaction costs shown by World App. Monitoring and automation are best-effort and can be delayed by funding, network or service availability.
