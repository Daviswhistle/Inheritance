Yield vault terms

Updated October 2, 2026 · Operated by Daviswhistle

These terms apply only if you choose an available Morpho yield vault and approve its creation. Basic WLD vaults remain separate and charge no platform fee. This page alone does not mean yield vault deposits are available.

Where your WLD goes

Approved deposits supply WLD to the fixed Re7 WLD ERC-4626 vault on World Chain. Your personal inheritance contract holds its receipt shares. The strategy, service fee rate and fee recipient are fixed at creation; the service has no upgrade or strategy-switch power. Morpho and the underlying vault curator retain their own protocol roles and risks.

Service fee

The creation screen shows the fixed rate, which cannot exceed 10%. There is no principal, entry, periodic management or inheritance-principal fee. The fee applies when fee-bearing receipt shares leave your vault, to their positive WLD gain above remaining deposited capital, including WLD campaign rewards delivered by the canonical distributor, whether claimed through the app or by an approved external operator. Underlying vault fees have already reduced lending gains. Remaining capital and closed losses must be recovered before later gains are charged; additional deposits increase the capital threshold. Previously paid fees are not refunded if a later exit loses value.

Unprocessed reward cash is also fee-bearing on withdrawal or inheritance, after proven closed-loss recovery. Received rewards can be reinvested without a rewards API proof. On cash redemption, the fee is paid in WLD and deducted from the gross withdrawal. On a receipt-share exit or inheritance, it is paid in shares using their quoted WLD value at exit. Share value is an estimate, not guaranteed redeemable cash. If that quote is unavailable, that exit's share fee is waived. Rounding is in favor of the user. Subsequent redemption of wallet-held shares charges no second service fee.

Example at a 10% service fee: depositing 100 WLD and later redeeming 110 WLD after underlying fees produces a 1 WLD service fee and a 109 WLD user payout. The same calculation applies if the gain is a claimed WLD campaign reward. At or below deposited capital after loss recovery, the fee is zero. Ordinary WLD gifts and unsolicited receipt shares are not fee-bearing deposits. Canonical campaign rewards are tracked separately from gifts. After inheritance, even externally claimed rewards are paid to the fixed inheritance recipient; an owner recovery cannot redirect them.

Campaign rewards

Available WLD rewards can be claimed using a proof from Merkl's API. The fixed canonical distributor validates the proof and sends rewards to your vault. Until inheritance completes, the received WLD is reinvested without increasing deposited principal or resetting the countdown. Reinvestment and claiming are one transaction; if the deposit or minimum-share check fails, the claim is canceled as well. Reward claims are separate from lending interest and require a transaction. Unpublished rewards cannot yet be claimed, and neither rewards nor a rate are guaranteed. This vault does not automatically qualify for World App's verified-human boost.

Rewards published after inheritance completes go directly to its fixed recipient after the same net-gain fee and any carried loss recovery. Anyone can relay a valid proof, but cannot select a different token, distributor or recipient. Releasing an empty vault clears the owner's current slot, while the known vault remains available for delayed rewards and heir claims. Keep its vault link. Reward publication or lookup failures never prevent principal inheritance.

Withdrawals and inheritance

Cash withdrawals can be delayed or fail when Morpho liquidity is insufficient. While your owner controls are available, you may instead transfer receipt shares to your wallet and redeem them later. After expiry, the named heir files a claim and waits seven days. The owner can renew until inheritance executes. An eligible inheritance attempts cash redemption; if redemption fails, it transfers receipt shares and idle WLD to the named heir. This completes inheritance without guaranteeing immediate cash. Network execution limits can also cause a share payout even when some liquidity exists.

Risks

Funds can lose value, including deposited principal. Smart contract failures, bad debt, oracle failure, curator allocation, token-price changes and insufficient liquidity can result in partial or total loss or delayed access. Neither a quoted yield nor receipt-share value is a guarantee. Protocol audits do not audit this integration; this new integration has no independent third-party security audit.

Choosing or moving funds

Existing vaults are not converted automatically. Creation and every deposit or owner withdrawal require your wallet approval. Choosing a yield vault accepts its disclosed fee and lending risks. The app supports a basic vault and a separate yield vault; each factory allows one current vault per owner. The operator pays automated execution gas from a dedicated ETH wallet; network fees may apply to your transactions as disclosed by World App.
