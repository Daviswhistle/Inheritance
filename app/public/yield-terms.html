Yield vault terms

Updated November 9, 2026 · Operated by Daviswhistle

These terms apply to the Morpho investment strategies used by default for new WLD and USDC plans. Existing free basic WLD vaults follow the general terms.

What is supplied

A WLD yield vault supplies deposits to Re7 WLD on Morpho. A USDC yield vault supplies World Chain USDC to Re7 USDC on Morpho. USDC principal and interest remain USDC; campaign rewards are WLD. The app does not convert between currencies. Each vault’s strategy, service fee and fee recipient are fixed when it is created and cannot be changed by the operator. The underlying Morpho vault has separate governance, curator, allocation and fee risks.

Our 10% performance fee

There is no principal or periodic management charge. A cash exit charges 10% of positive realized gains after the underlying vault's costs. A receipt-share exit charges the corresponding positive-gain fee in shares, using their quoted underlying-asset value. A failed valuation waives that share-exit's fee. Deposited capital and direct token or receipt-share gifts are not fee-bearing. Fees already paid are not refunded if later losses occur.

USDC and WLD are accounted separately, without conversion or offsets between currencies. In a USDC vault, realized USDC losses offset later realized USDC gains before a USDC fee is collected. Official WLD rewards received by a USDC vault carry a separate 10% WLD fee when distributed. A USDC loss does not offset a WLD reward fee. For example, a 100 USDC deposit exited at 110 USDC pays 1 USDC; 10 claimed WLD rewards pay 1 WLD. WLD gifts pay no fee.

In a WLD yield vault, claimed WLD rewards can be reinvested without increasing deposited capital. Remaining capital and realized WLD losses offset WLD gains before the fee. Partial withdrawals allocate capital and gains proportionally. Receipt shares redeemed later from your wallet have already paid their service fee on leaving the inheritance vault and are not charged a second service fee.

Underlying fees and displayed rates

The underlying Re7 vault charges its own performance fee and can change its fees and allocations. The app reads the current strategy fee from its contract before deposit. Our fee is additional and applies after the underlying costs. Displayed lending APR and general WLD rewards APR are separate, dated estimates before our exit fee. Rewards eligibility, exchange values, rate sources and campaign end dates can change. An annualized reward rate is not a promise of that year's payout.

WLD campaign rewards

Campaign participation is not guaranteed. World App's verified-human boost is not automatic for a personal vault contract and is excluded from our general-rate display. Rewards become claimable only after publication by the canonical Merkl distributor.

Active WLD yield vaults reinvest available claimed WLD. Active USDC vaults keep claimed WLD separately. Once its reward accounting is verified, the owner can withdraw this WLD separately while owner controls remain available or receive it with a full cash exit. Partial USDC or receipt-share exits leave WLD in the vault. Reward claims do not renew the countdown. After inheritance, newly claimed or externally delivered canonical WLD rewards go to the fixed inheritance recipient after the applicable fee; they cannot be reclaimed by the original owner. Keep the vault link so rewards published after inheritance can still be claimed.

Collecting income

New supporting contracts let the owner collect available lending income without withdrawing tracked inheritance capital. Available income is calculated after accounting for remaining deposited capital and prior realized losses, with a 10% fee on positive gains actually realized. Income collection does not reset the check-in timer or change the heir. Quotes depend on current strategy valuation and cash liquidity; principal remains subject to the risks below. Existing contracts retain their original withdrawal controls.

Withdrawals and inheritance

Cash withdrawals depend on Morpho liquidity. The owner can move receipt shares instead and redeem them later as liquidity allows. After expiry, the named heir must file a claim and wait seven days. The owner can renew to cancel the claim until execution. An eligible inheritance attempts cash redemption and falls back to receipt shares if redemption fails. The fixed heir receives idle underlying assets and, in a USDC vault, held WLD rewards after their separate fee, subject to the WLD reconciliation described below. Receipt shares may have to be held until liquidity becomes available. Transaction execution limits can cause a share payout even when some liquidity exists.

If the on-chain reward counter is unavailable, supporting contracts can release invested assets first while retaining WLD until its reward accounting can be reconciled. After inheritance executes with WLD deferred, all WLD held until reconciliation, including gifts received during that period, belongs to the fixed heir and cannot be recovered by the original owner. The reward fee applies only to canonical claimed rewards; gifts remain fee-free. Receipt of the invested assets does not mean the WLD settlement is complete. Once the counter is available, deferred inheritance WLD can be paid through the app's reward settlement action.

Risks

Deposited principal can be lost. Contract failures, bad debt, oracle failure, allocation changes, token-price changes and insufficient liquidity can cause partial or total loss or delayed access. USDC can lose its dollar peg or be subject to issuer restrictions; USDC is not a bank deposit. A receipt quote and a displayed rate do not guarantee value or liquidity.

Your choices and transaction costs

Creating a vault, adding funds and owner withdrawals require wallet approval. Funds move only when the approved transaction executes. Existing WLD vaults are not converted or migrated automatically. Each asset vault has its own balance, heir and countdown. When creating new vaults for both WLD and USDC, each asset has a separate wallet approval. Creation and deposit for that asset execute together. Cancelling a later approval leaves earlier completed deposits in their vaults; only the remaining assets need to be resumed. Each transaction may have its own network fee. The operator pays automatic inheritance execution costs. World App may charge a network fee in WLD when gas sponsorship is unavailable. Vault creation, additional deposits and withdrawals can each incur this cost, separately from service and strategy fees. Network fees can also apply when reusing a vault. Review the exact quote in World App before approving. Monitoring and automation are best-effort and can be delayed by funding, network or service availability.
