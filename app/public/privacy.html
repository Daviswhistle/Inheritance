Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026 · Operated by Daviswhistle

Inheritance is a WLD and USDC transfer planning app for World App. This page explains what our app and notification service use. Public blockchain records can identify wallet activity even when a username is not shown.

Your choices before data use

Before requesting a wallet signature, the sign-in screen explains which wallet data is used and links to this policy and the Terms. The one-hour session and storage details are described below. Continuing requests your agreement; you can browse Help without signing in. Creating a plan separately authorizes vault monitoring and device recovery storage as explained on the final review. Contact access and notification permission are requested separately in World App. Do not enter another person's private information; recipient addresses and on-chain transfers are public.

Wallet sign-in and display

World App provides your wallet address and, when available, username. We verify your sign-in message and signature to issue a session that expires after one hour. The session is stored in this browser tab, not a permanent login cookie. We store a hash of a random, one-use sign-in nonce for up to ten minutes and delete it when used; expired nonce and request-limit records are periodically cleared. To limit sign-in request bursts, we use your connection IP only in memory to create a secret-keyed, minute-specific hash. Our database stores that opaque hash and a request count, not the raw IP; the limit expires after one minute. We do not store your sign-in signature as an account record.

Recovery information on this device

To check interrupted setup and transactions and prevent duplicate requests, this device stores recovery information in browser storage. This includes wallet and recipient addresses, network and original contract addresses, assets, request reference blocks, requested amounts, fees, minimum proceeds, and request progress or transaction identifiers, as applicable to each action. Setup records remain after the app closes and are cleared after verified completion or when an editable setup is discarded; a completion record remains on the device to prevent older setup copies from being restored. Deposit and owner-withdrawal records also remain after the app closes and are cleared after verified completion, definite rejection or confirmed failure. An uncertain result remains recorded and blocks further deposits or withdrawals of the same type until checked. Unconfirmed withdrawals are not sent again automatically. Recipient and interval changes keep recovery records only in the current browser tab. Ending inheritance and withdrawing all assets saves recovery information across app closures until the original outcome, definite rejection or confirmed failure is verified; uncertain requests are retained and are not sent again automatically. The relevant review screens explain device storage before approval. Clearing app or site data removes these records and may prevent checking interrupted requests or avoiding duplicates. Public candidate addresses for received inheritances are also remembered on this device and verified again when opened.

Vault monitoring and automatic transfers

Our service stores registered vault, owner and heir addresses, active monitoring status, public vault balances, check timestamps, notification attempts and delivery results. When an authenticated current heir opens a verified plan, we also record the visit time and their reported notification permission (enabled, disabled or unknown). The owner can see this last observed readiness; it does not guarantee notification delivery. Completed yield records retain the contract-verified payout recipient and completion notification result so the recipient can reopen their history. These records let us send reminders and discover registered vaults. For new vaults, the service also stores claim identifiers, signed execution transactions, transaction hashes and gas spending records so it can recover interrupted submissions without duplicate transfers. These transactions are signed by the operator’s execution wallet. We do not store users’ private keys.

Contacts and notifications

Contact selection happens in World App. We use only the selected contact's wallet address and username in the app; we do not import or store your full address book. Permission is needed for World App notifications, and your heir must enable notifications separately. Notification delivery is processed by World App and can fail or be delayed.

Infrastructure

Cloudflare hosts the app, authentication and monitoring database. Public World Chain RPC services receive wallet and contract queries. For a selected yield vault, the browser sends that vault's public address to Merkl to look up WLD campaign rewards and proofs; it does not send a wallet signature, username or private key. World App and its developer APIs process wallet commands, usernames, transaction status and notification requests under their own policies. Hosting providers may process connection information and operational logs. We do not include advertising trackers or analytics in this release.

What we do not collect

  • Your wallet private key, seed phrase or biometric data.
  • A copy of your full address book.

No World ID verification is required in this release.

Retention and requests

Monitoring records remain while a vault is registered, including inactive records needed to avoid repeated alerts. You may ask us to stop monitoring or delete off-chain records by contacting support; we may ask for proof that you control the relevant wallet. If monitoring stops, automated execution and reminders for that vault may stop. Blockchain transactions and their public history cannot be deleted by us. Access or correction requests can also be sent to support. Do not send keys or seed phrases.
