Privacy Policy

Updated November 4, 2026 · Operated by Daviswhistle

Inheritance is a WLD and USDC transfer planning app for World App. This page explains what our app and notification service use. Public blockchain records can identify wallet activity even when a username is not shown.

Your choices before data use

Before requesting a wallet signature, the sign-in screen explains which wallet data is used and links to this policy and the Terms. The one-hour session and storage details are described below. Continuing requests your agreement; you can browse Help without signing in. Creating a plan separately authorizes vault monitoring and device recovery storage as explained on the final review. Contact access and notification permission are requested separately in World App. Do not enter another person's private information; recipient addresses and on-chain transfers are public.

Wallet sign-in and display

World App provides your wallet address and, when available, username. We verify your sign-in message and signature to issue a session that expires after one hour. The session is stored in this browser tab, not a permanent login cookie. We store a hash of a random, one-use sign-in nonce for up to ten minutes and delete it when used; expired nonce and request-limit records are periodically cleared. To limit sign-in request bursts, we use your connection IP only in memory to create a secret-keyed, minute-specific hash. Our database stores that opaque hash and a request count, not the raw IP; the limit expires after one minute. We do not store your sign-in signature as an account record. To recover interrupted plan setup after the app closes, this device stores your chosen heir, amounts, original contract targets and request progress in persistent browser storage. The setup record is cleared after verified completion or when an editable setup is discarded; an empty completion marker remains to prevent older copies from restoring it. Clearing app or site data removes these recovery records and can prevent recovery of an interrupted request. Additional deposits also keep a persistent device recovery record of the wallet, chain, factory, vault, token, exact amount, request boundary and returned transaction identifier. The deposit review explains this storage before approval. A verified completion, definite rejection or confirmed failure clears the record; an uncertain result stays recorded and blocks another deposit until checked. It is never treated as success from a balance increase alone. Clearing site data removes this protection. Owner withdrawals from Assets also retain the wallet, chain, original factory and vault, token, recipient, requested amount, minimum proceeds and request identifier on this device before wallet approval. Their review discloses this storage. An unconfirmed withdrawal blocks further withdrawals until its original receipt, definite rejection or confirmed failure is verified; it is never repeated automatically. Clearing app or site data removes this protection. Recipient and interval changes keep their recovery records in the current browser tab. Ending inheritance and withdrawing all assets instead saves a recovery record on this device across app closures: your wallet and recipient addresses, original contracts, reviewed amounts and fees, and transaction progress or identifiers. The exit review explains this before you continue. The record is deleted after the original outcome is verified, or after a definite rejection or failure. An uncertain request is retained for checking; it is not sent again automatically. Clearing app or site data removes this record. Public candidate addresses for received inheritances are remembered in browser storage and verified again when opened.

New-plan availability and residence choice

Before the official interface sends a new vault or deposit request, it asks a read-only Cloudflare Pages endpoint for the country code supplied in the request's trusted Cloudflare country field. The endpoint does not use caller-supplied location headers or query parameters, store the request IP, or write to an app database. Cloudflare may process connection information under its hosting practices. The app also stores your selected supported residence country and confirmation in browser storage for that wallet; it is not sent to our server. This is a self-attestation paired with an approximate network-location check. It does not prove residence, reliably detect VPN use, block direct immutable-contract calls, or provide KYC or sanctions screening. Sign-in and access to existing vaults and safety actions remain available.

Vault monitoring and automatic transfers

Our service stores registered vault, owner and heir addresses, active monitoring status, public vault balances, check timestamps, notification attempts and delivery results. When an authenticated current heir opens a verified plan, we also record the visit time and their reported notification permission (enabled, disabled or unknown). The owner can see this last observed readiness; it does not guarantee notification delivery. Completed yield records retain the contract-verified payout recipient and completion notification result so the recipient can reopen their history. These records let us send reminders and discover registered vaults. For new vaults, the service also stores claim identifiers, signed execution transactions, transaction hashes and gas spending records so it can recover interrupted submissions without duplicate transfers. These transactions are signed by our gas-only service wallet, never by a stored user key. The execution key pays network gas; it cannot choose a different recipient or withdraw an active owner's funds.

Contacts and notifications

Contact selection happens in World App. We use only the selected contact's wallet address and username in the app; we do not import or store your full address book. Permission is needed for World App notifications, and your heir must enable notifications separately. Notification delivery is processed by World App and can fail or be delayed.

Infrastructure

Cloudflare hosts the app, authentication and monitoring database. Public World Chain RPC services receive wallet and contract queries. For a selected yield vault, the browser sends that vault's public address to Merkl to look up WLD campaign rewards and proofs; it does not send a wallet signature, username or private key. World App and its developer APIs process wallet commands, usernames, transaction status and notification requests under their own policies. Hosting providers may process connection information and operational logs. We do not include advertising trackers or analytics in this release.

What we do not collect

  • Your wallet private key, seed phrase or biometric data.
  • A copy of your full address book.
  • Custody of your WLD or USDC. Deposited funds are held by your on-chain vault or its fixed strategy.

No World ID verification is required in this release.

Retention and requests

Monitoring records remain while a vault is registered, including inactive records needed to avoid repeated alerts. You may ask us to stop monitoring or delete off-chain records by contacting support; we may ask for proof that you control the relevant wallet. If monitoring stops, automated execution and reminders for that vault may stop. Blockchain transactions and their public history cannot be deleted by us. Access or correction requests can also be sent to support. Do not send keys or seed phrases.
