How the vault works

  1. You choose an heir's wallet address, a check-in period and WLD and/or USDC amounts. After you review and approve, the app batches any required asset-contract creation and the chosen deposits into one wallet request. It manages the asset contracts together as one plan.
  2. You can renew the timer. Changing the heir or period and owner withdrawals follow the contract's rules. If the timer has already expired, renew first to restore active-owner controls.
  3. After expiry, only the named heir may file a claim. A seven-day waiting period begins. The owner can renew to cancel the pending claim at any time before the transfer executes.
  4. After the waiting period, a registered new vault is eligible for automated transfer to its named heir. No further heir signature is required for that transfer. The heir may also complete it manually in World App. Older vaults that do not support automation require manual completion.

Automatic execution is a best-effort service. Network conditions, gas funding, service availability and monitoring registration can delay or stop it. Eligibility at seven days is not a guarantee of transfer at an exact time. Transaction ordering determines whether a renewal or eligible transfer takes effect first.
