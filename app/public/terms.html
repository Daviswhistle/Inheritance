How the vault works

  1. Choose the heir’s wallet address, a check-in period and the WLD or USDC amounts to deposit in the vault, then review and approve in your wallet. The app groups the asset vaults in one plan.
  2. Tap “Check in” on Home and approve in World App. Once the transaction completes, the chosen period starts again. Changing the heir or period and owner withdrawals follow the contract's rules. If the timer has already expired, renew first to restore active-owner controls.
  3. After expiry, only the named heir may file a claim. A seven-day waiting period begins. The owner can renew to cancel the pending claim at any time before the transfer executes.
  4. After the waiting period, a registered new vault is eligible for automated transfer to its named heir. No further heir signature is required for that transfer. The heir may also complete it manually in World App. Older vaults that do not support automation require manual completion.

Automatic transfers may be delayed or interrupted by network or service conditions, insufficient execution funding or registration status. Passing the seven-day waiting period does not guarantee an exact transfer time. Transaction ordering determines whether a renewal or eligible transfer takes effect first.
