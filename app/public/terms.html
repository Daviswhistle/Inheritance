A transfer plan, not a legal will.

This app follows a timer and wallet signatures. It does not verify death, identity, legal inheritance rights or incapacity. A missed check-in can make a claim eligible while you are alive. Use an appropriate legal estate plan where needed.

How the vault works

  1. You choose an heir's wallet address and a check-in period, then deposit WLD or USDC into its configured vault. Creating a vault moves no funds.
  2. You can renew the timer. Changing the heir or period and owner withdrawals follow the contract's rules. If the timer has already expired, renew first to restore active-owner controls.
  3. After expiry, only the named heir may file a claim. A seven-day review window begins. The owner can renew to cancel the pending claim at any time before the transfer executes.
  4. After the review window, a registered new vault is eligible for automated transfer to its named heir. No further heir signature is required for that transfer. The heir may also complete it manually in World App. Older vaults that do not support automation require manual completion.

Automatic execution is a best-effort service. Network conditions, gas funding, service availability and monitoring registration can delay or stop it. Eligibility at seven days is not a guarantee of transfer at an exact time. Transaction ordering determines whether a renewal or eligible transfer takes effect first.
